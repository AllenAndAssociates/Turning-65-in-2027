# Allen & Associates Brand

## Logo

| File | Use |
|---|---|
| `assets/logo.png` | Original, 577×181, on white. Default choice. |
| `assets/logo_transparent.png` | White removed, for placing on white/very light backgrounds (cards, light stages). |

Paths are relative to this skill folder: `.claude/skills/allen-associates-brand/assets/`.

Rules:
- Use the supplied logo file; never recreate, redraw, re-type or recolor it, and never use a monogram or "A&A" stand-in.
- Place it on **white or a very light background only**. The wordmark is blue and green, so it disappears on navy, blue or green. On a dark design, put the logo on a white rounded panel.
- Keep the aspect ratio (≈3.19 : 1). Leave clear space around it of at least the height of the "MAKING MEDICARE EASY" line.
- The source is 577 px wide; avoid displaying it much wider than ~700 px (it will soften).
- Lockup content: icon (blue/green cross) + "ALLEN &" (green) + "ASSOCIATES" (blue) + "INSURANCE AGENCY" (green) + tagline "MAKING MEDICARE EASY" (charcoal).

## Colors

| Role | Name | Hex | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Primary | Brand Blue | `#0072BC` | "ASSOCIATES", icon blue |
| Primary | Brand Green | `#4CAF2E` | "ALLEN &", icon green |
| Light tint | Sky Blue | `#0096E6` | top of the blue gradient |
| Light tint | Lime Green | `#8DC21F` | top of the green gradient |
| Dark | Deep Blue | `#00579A` | backgrounds, headers, text that needs contrast |
| Neutral | Charcoal | `#303030` | tagline, body text |
| Neutral | White | `#FFFFFF` | logo background, cards |

Gradients in the logo run light → dark, top-left to bottom-right: Blue `#0096E6 → #0068B0`, Green `#8DC21F → #3EA535`.

Usage:
- Lead with Brand Blue and Brand Green on white. Use Deep Blue for large dark areas (banners, contact cards) and Brand Green as the accent (borders, highlights, checkmarks, key numbers).
- White text on Deep Blue or Brand Blue; Charcoal or Deep Blue text on white. White text on Brand Green only at large, bold sizes.
- Red is not a brand color; use it only for warnings/deadlines (e.g. a "NOT FOR YOU" stamp).

## Type

The logo uses a rounded geometric sans-serif. In designs, use a clean geometric/grotesk sans (e.g. Inter / Inter Display, Montserrat, Century Gothic) in bold or black weights for headlines, with wide letter-spacing for small caps lines like the tagline.
